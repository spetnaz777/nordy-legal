1. The short version

Nordy is built to keep your data on your phone.

Your habits, quests, focus sessions, XP and level, streaks, oaths, calendar entries and Ward (Screen Time) rules are stored on your device. They are not uploaded to us. We do not operate a server that receives, stores, or analyses your Nordy activity, and we cannot see it.

We run no advertising, no tracking, no data brokers, and no third-party analytics SDKs. We do not sell or share your personal information, as those terms are defined under US state privacy laws. Our App Store privacy manifest declares no tracking, and that is accurate.

Information leaves your device in three cases only: when you create an account with an email address and password, which our authentication provider stores so you can sign in again and reset a forgotten password (section 4.4); when you choose to sign in with Apple, Google, or Discord; and when you make a purchase, which Apple handles. Those are described in section 4.

What we hold about you is your email address and an account identifier — never your habits, your history, or anything you do inside the app.

2. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to the Nordy iOS application and any page we publish at spetnaz777.github.io/nordy-legal.

Nordy is intended for users aged 13 and over (16 and over in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, or the higher age set by your country's law). Nordy is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children below those ages. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact nordydevapp@gmail.com and we will delete it.

3. What is stored on your device only

The following stay in Nordy's private storage area on your device. We have no copy and no access:

  • Habits and quests, their schedules, targets, categories and check-in history
  • Focus sessions: length, start time, completion, and any habit a session was bound to
  • Progress data: XP, level, rank, streaks, achievements, challenges
  • Oaths and their outcomes
  • Calendar entries you create inside Nordy
  • Ward configuration and its counters (see section 6)
  • Your app preferences: theme, day start hour, reminder settings, sound settings
  • Your display name, if you enter one
  • If you create an email account inside Nordy: your email address, and a cryptographic hash of your password (see section 8)

Uninstalling Nordy removes this data from your device. Depending on your settings, a copy may exist in your own iCloud or iTunes device backup, which is controlled by you and by Apple, not by us.

4. What can leave your device, and only when you choose it

4.1 Signing in with Apple, Google, or Discord

If you choose one of these, you are sent to that provider to authenticate. We never receive your password for those accounts.

  • Sign in with Apple — we receive the identifier Apple issues, and the name and email address you approve. If you use Apple's Hide My Email, we only ever see the relay address.
  • Google — Nordy requests the profile and email scopes and reads your basic profile and email address from Google's userinfo endpoint.
  • Discord — Nordy requests the identify and email scopes and reads your basic account information and email address from Discord.

What Nordy receives is stored on your device and used to identify your account inside the app. Your use of those providers is governed by their own privacy policies, and their receipt of your authentication request is between you and them.

4.2 Purchases

Subscriptions and any other purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Nordy never receives, handles, or stores your payment card, billing address, or Apple ID credentials. We receive only the entitlement status needed to unlock paid features. Apple's handling of your purchase is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

4.3 Diagnostics

Nordy ships no third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Product events exist in the code as named milestones but are written to the developer console in development builds only and are silent in the version you download. Nothing is transmitted.

If we later add analytics or crash reporting, we will update this policy, name the provider, and — where the law requires consent — ask you first.

4.4 Your account

If you create an account with an email address and password, that account is held by Supabase, which provides our authentication service. This is what lets you sign in again on the same device and reset a password you have forgotten — neither is possible for an account that exists only on one phone.

  • What is sent: your email address, your chosen display name, and your password. Your password is transmitted over an encrypted connection and stored by Supabase as a salted hash; neither we nor Supabase can read it back.
  • What comes back: an account identifier and a session token, which is kept in your device's Keychain.
  • What is not sent: your habits, quests, focus sessions, streaks, oaths, calendar entries, Ward rules, and Screen Time selections. Those remain on your device, exactly as described in section 3. Signing in on a new device gives you your account, not your history.

Verification and reset emails are delivered by Resend on our behalf. To send them, Resend processes your email address and the message itself.

Supabase and Resend act as our processors: they handle this information on our instructions and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

If you create an account with Apple, Google, or Discord instead, or continue as a guest, no account is created with Supabase and nothing in this section applies to you.

5. Notifications

Nordy's reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device by the app itself. They are not push notifications, we do not operate a push server, and no notification content is transmitted to us. You can revoke notification permission at any time in iOS Settings, and you can turn individual reminders off inside Nordy.

While a Focus session is running, Nordy cancels its own reminders so they do not interrupt you, and restores them when the session ends. Nordy cannot silence notifications from other apps; only your device's Do Not Disturb or Focus settings can do that.

6. The Ward and Screen Time data (important)

Nordy's Ward uses Apple's Family Controls, Device Activity and Managed Settings frameworks to help you shield distracting apps.

By Apple's design and requirement:

  • The apps and categories you select are represented to Nordy as opaque tokens. Nordy cannot read which specific apps you chose, cannot see their names, and cannot enumerate what is installed on your device.
  • Screen Time and Device Activity information never leaves your device, is never transmitted to us, and is not used for any purpose other than operating the Ward feature you enabled.
  • We do not use this data for advertising or profiling, and we do not share it with anyone.

Granting Screen Time authorization is entirely your choice, and you can withdraw it at any time in iOS Settings, which disables the Ward.

The Ward is a self-control aid, not a security control. It is subject to the limits of Apple's frameworks and of your device's settings, and it can be turned off by anyone who can unlock your device. Do not rely on it as a safety mechanism for anyone else.

7. Legal bases for processing (EEA and UK)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on:

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) — operating your account and providing the features you asked for.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — keeping the app secure and preventing abuse. We have balanced this against your rights and it is limited to what is necessary.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — where you grant notification or Screen Time permission, and for any future optional processing. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) — where we must retain or disclose information by law.

Because your content stays on your device, most of what you do in Nordy involves no processing by us at all.

8. How we protect information

  • Passwords for Nordy email accounts are never stored in readable form. Supabase stores only a salted hash. The copy kept on your device — which lets you sign in when you have no connection — is salted and stretched with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA-256, and only the derived hash is written. Verification uses a comparison designed not to leak information through timing.
  • Your session token is held in the device Keychain, which is encrypted by iOS and excluded from unencrypted backups.
  • Data in transit for the flows in section 4 uses HTTPS/TLS. The app ships with App Transport Security enabled and does not permit arbitrary insecure loads.
  • Nordy requests no microphone, camera, contacts, photos, precise location, or health data, and declares no such permissions.

No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Because your data lives on your device, the security of your device — its passcode, biometrics, and backups — is the most important protection, and it is under your control.

9. Retention

On-device data is retained until you delete it in the app or uninstall Nordy.

Your account record with Supabase — email address, display name, password hash — is retained until you delete your account. Settings → Delete account erases it both on your device and at Supabase; it is not recoverable afterwards. Verification and reset emails handled by Resend are retained by that service only as long as needed to deliver them and to guard against abuse.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal information, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Residents of California and other US states with comparable laws may have rights to know, delete, correct, and to opt out of sale or sharing — we do not sell or share personal information, and we do not process it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any right.

For most data, you can exercise these rights directly and immediately:

  • Delete everything — Settings → Delete account, inside Nordy, which erases your app data from the device and deletes your account record at Supabase.
  • Correct or export — edit any entry in the app; contact us if you need an export.

For anything held by us, contact nordydevapp@gmail.com. We will respond within the period required by applicable law (generally one month under the GDPR, 45 days under California law). You also have the right to complain to your local supervisory authority; in the UK this is the Information Commissioner's Office.
